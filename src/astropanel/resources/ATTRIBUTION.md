The bundled `world_land.svg` is generated from Natural Earth 1:110m land
polygons. Natural Earth data is public domain. It is rendered locally and is
not a map-service dependency.

The lunar observing panel downloads hourly phase/libration renderings and
matching feature-label layers from NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio,
based on LRO/LOLA/LROC data. NASA SVS Dial-A-Moon provides the date, frame and
annual collection. The panel combines the plain Moon image and label layer
for the same frame, cropped to the central lunar disk.
2026 collection: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/5587/
Credits: NASA/GSFC Scientific Visualization Studio, Ernie Wright, Noah Petro.
